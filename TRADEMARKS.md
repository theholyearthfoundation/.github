## Two marks

<img src="profile/assets/builders-mark.svg" width="32" height="32" alt="The builders' mark: the Eagle, the Condor and the Owl in a golden halo" align="right">

**💛 The golden heart is the foundation's mark.** A heart in a circle of light, with vine leaves:
it's Isaiah (Kenney)'s, and it's the one the foundation shows the world. It's the icon of every
site, it sits at the heart of the profile banner's Golden Halo, and it's on the share cards. Where
the foundation speaks, the heart is there.

**🦅🦉 The three birds are the builders' mark.** The Eagle (A Course in Miracles), the Condor (the
plant medicine traditions) and the Owl between them (the two held as one): the Plants & Miracles
teaching, drawn small when the foundation's GitHub portal began. It never stands in front of the
heart. It marks the places where the work is made:

- the members' edition of the profile, beside its notice;
- the browser tabs of the lab and rehearsal sites, so a builder always knows which version is open;
- the "how this was made" corners: credits, changelogs, notes for helpers.

The file is [`profile/assets/builders-mark.svg`](profile/assets/builders-mark.svg). Both marks
belong to the foundation, and everything above applies to both.
