# 🎞️ Decks

Slide decks for The Holy Earth Foundation, made with [Marp](https://marp.app). Each deck comes as three
files: the **PDF** for your eyes, the **HTML** to present (download it, then open it in a browser), and the
**markdown** it was made from, for your AI helper.

| Deck | What it walks through | Files |
|---|---|---|
| **Welcome to The Holy Earth Foundation** | for any newcomer and the AI helper beside them, in seven stops: your first hour (SOP-COM-001), five words, proposals, procedures, the reading room and your helper, how a change travels, how to answer | [PDF](welcome.marp.pdf) · [HTML](welcome.marp.html) · [source](welcome.marp.md) |
| **Home base**, for Kenney | members only: the foundation's GitHub signed in, the reading room and your helper, the proposals waiting | [in the reading room](https://github.com/theholyearthfoundation/reading-room/tree/main/decks) (opens for signed-in members) |

**Where else to read:**
- [The reading room](https://theholyearthfoundation.github.io/reading-room-public/): proposals, procedures and decks the foundation is ready to show.
- [The procedures' public twin](https://github.com/theholyearthfoundation/ops-standard-operating-procedures-public): the SOPs marked public.
- Members read everything in [`reading-room`](https://github.com/theholyearthfoundation/reading-room), [`proposals`](https://github.com/theholyearthfoundation/proposals) and [`ops-standard-operating-procedures`](https://github.com/theholyearthfoundation/ops-standard-operating-procedures) while signed in.

<sub>Render a deck: `marp --no-stdin --html --allow-local-files <deck>.marp.md -o <deck>.marp.html`, then the same with `--pdf -o <deck>.marp.pdf`, and look at the pages before committing. The welcome deck is filled from [my-template's decks-template](https://github.com/drasticstatic/my-template/tree/main/decks-template) (redesigned and re-filled 2026-11-01, Marp 4.3.1). Home base lives in the members' reading room, not here: this shelf is for decks anyone may read.</sub>
